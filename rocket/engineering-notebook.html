Project notes

Week of October 18–24, 2026

Finishing the aft assembly

We started the week by post-processing the cut G10 fiberglass fins. I sanded them with 240-grit sandpaper and shaped them into a bullnose profile.

  • We measured the fins’ mass at 314 grams. The aft assembly was slightly too heavy, which may negatively affect stability, so we need to add more mass to the nose cone.
  • We designed a body-tube cutting jig in Fusion, 3D-printed it in PETG, and taped off the cutting area. The rigid jig guided a freehand cut with a Dremel rotary tool, and the cut came out clean.
  • We bonded the centering rings, motor holder, and fins with JB Weld epoxy, then left the assembly upside down to cure for 24 hours.

Next: 3D-print a guide with the correct fillet radius, then epoxy the aft assembly to the body tube.
